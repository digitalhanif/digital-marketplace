# Agent Operating Rules
Project: Digital Marketplace
Author: Muhammad Hanif Haroon
Read SKILL.md, business configuration and the relevant workflow before research. Keep research evidence, assumptions and recommendations separate. Use only compliant data access. Apply evidence, compliance and QA policies. Prefer the USA buyer market and USD pricing. Support digital products and Etsy-eligible physical products.
