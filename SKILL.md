# Digital Marketplace
Owner: Muhammad Hanif Haroon | Version: 1.0.0

## When to use
Activate for Etsy market discovery, niche research, product opportunity evaluation, pricing, SEO, sourcing feasibility, and launch reviews.

## Operating contract
1. Read `AGENTS.md` and `config/business-profile.yaml` before planning.
2. Choose a workflow in `workflows/` and consult only the relevant agent instructions in `agents/`.
3. Collect current, attributed observations. Record source URL, access date, geography, observation type, and confidence. Never invent sales, keyword volumes, listing counts or conversion rates.
4. Separate directly observed facts, third-party estimates, and recommendations.
5. Apply compliance and evidence policies before scoring or advising sourcing.
6. Score candidates with seven ratings on a 0–10 scale; report evidence confidence separately.
7. Provide ranked shortlist, differentiation, costs, sources, launch experiment, and BUILD/TEST/WATCH/SKIP decision.
8. Obtain user approval before publishing, buying, contacting vendors or changing shop listings.

## Independence and risk disclaimer
This is an independent educational/research tool, not an official Etsy product or Etsy representative. Never imply Etsy affiliation, authorization or endorsement. Explain that estimates and research scores do not guarantee sales, income or profit. Users are responsible for commercial decisions and losses; read `DISCLAIMER.md` and `LICENSE` before use.

## Output standards
Explicitly label missing evidence and freshness. No guarantees of sales or profitability.
